The Arrakis Intelligence Platform extension collects and processes user-provided content, webpage content selected by the user, authentication information required for account access, and conversation history associated with the platform's AI features.

This information is used solely to provide AI-powered research, summarization, analysis, and productivity functionality. Data is transmitted securely to Arrakis backend services for processing and storage as required by the platform's features.

The extension does not sell user data and does not share personal information with third parties for advertising purposes.
